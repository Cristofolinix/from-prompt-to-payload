# Responsible use and ethical scope

This repository is the **reproducibility package** of an academic study on the **dual use of large language models (LLMs) in cybersecurity**, accompanying the paper *From Prompt to Payload: Refusal Bypass via System Instruction and the Validation of LLM-Generated Cybersecurity Artifacts* (RITA, 2026).

## Purpose

The material exists to **enable independent verification** of the results reported in the paper — nothing more. It documents an experiment conducted entirely in an **isolated laboratory**, built by the author, that abstracts the **Equifax (2017)** incident chain.

## Environment guarantees

- **Fully isolated laboratory**, with no connection to real or third-party systems. The reproduction virtual machine must be run **offline**.
- **Public, already-patched target:** the vulnerability reproduced is **CVE-2017-5638** (Apache Struts / S2-045), disclosed and patched since 2017 and distributed by the public Vulhub project. No novel or *zero-day* technique is introduced.
- **Fictitious data:** all credentials, customer files, and *flags* are invented and labeled as such. No real personal data is present.

## Usage limits

- The offensive artifacts in this repository **must not be run against any system you do not own or are not explicitly authorized to test.** Using them outside a controlled, authorized environment may be illegal.
- The artifacts were **generated by the evaluated models** and are published *verbatim* as an object of study — not as tools to be refined or operationalized.
- The ***jailbreak* configurations** used in the experiment **are not redistributed here**; the paper only references the public source repository, and this package does not include them.

## Disclosure

The CVE is public and patched; the study discloses no new vulnerability. The goal is defensive: to show, in a measurable and reproducible way, why containing the malicious use of LLMs is structurally difficult, in order to inform the security community.

If in doubt about the appropriate use of this material, contact the authors (see the *How to cite* section in the README).
